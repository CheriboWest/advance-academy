## Interview Prep — interview stages and aptitude tests

On the Interview tool, I like the part 'Choose the interview persona'. I think add a new part about 'Choose the interview stage'. Because the interview process is often divided into several rounds, each round with different department and people. The interview process is like this: Phone screening interview (often with an HR person) -> First interview with HR (some companies don't do the phone screening round and jump straight to the first interview)  -> 2nd interview with a senior-level member -> 3rd interview with key decision makers like head of the department, manager, director, CEO, etc. => Add this part before choosing the interview persona.

for each round of the interview process, there's often a fixed list of interview questions. It's not necessary that the candidate answers one question and the interviewer asks a follow-up question based on the candidate's answers. The questions could be entirely not related to the candidate's answers. Does that make sense?

One more thing, depending on the types of roles or industries, there'll be a round called 'Online Assessment' sandwiched between interview rounds. These online assessments are called  'Aptitude tests'. it can be varied depending on roles and industries. The test can be after the CV round (no interview round yet) or the test can be after the 1st or 2nd interview rounds to test the candidates' skills.
